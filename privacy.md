# Next PATCO Train Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Next PATCO Train does not collect personal data.

The app may use your device location, with your permission, to estimate whether you can reach your selected PATCO station and to orient your selected route. Location information is processed on your device and may be stored locally to support widgets. Location information is not sent to the developer, sold, shared, or used for tracking.

The app may use Apple Maps/MapKit services to calculate walking or driving estimates to a station. Those requests are handled by Apple according to Apple’s privacy practices.

The app displays public schedule, special schedule, and service advisory information. Schedule information may change without notice and does not reflect real-time train operations.

Next PATCO Train is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PATCO or the Delaware River Port Authority.

For support, questions, or feedback, please open an issue on this GitHub repository:

https://github.com/larryreeve/next_patco_train/issues
