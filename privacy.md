# Next PATCO Train Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Next PATCO Train does not send your device location or trip history to the developer.

## Optional feedback

If you choose Send Feedback, your message, feedback type, app version, and iOS version are sent to the developer through a third-party service provider that processes and delivers submissions.

The app does not attach your device location or trip history to feedback submissions. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in your message.


## Location information

The app may use your device location, with your permission, to estimate whether you can reach your selected PATCO station, orient your selected route, and estimate station progress and destination arrival during a selected trip.

Location information is processed on your device and may be stored locally to support widgets and trip progress. It is not sent to the developer, sold, or used for advertising tracking.

The app may use Apple Maps/MapKit services to calculate walking or driving estimates to a station. Those requests are handled by Apple according to Apple’s privacy practices.



## Schedule Information

The app displays public schedule, special schedule, and service advisory information provided by PATCO. Schedule information may change without notice and does not reflect real-time train operations.

Next PATCO Train is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PATCO or the Delaware River Port Authority.

## Feedback

For suggestions, please use the send feedback option in the app.
