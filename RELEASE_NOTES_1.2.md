# What's New in 1.2

Plan your PATCO trip with more confidence.

- Improved station detection and route direction while traveling.
- More reliable return-trip directions after leaving your destination station.
- Clearer departure guidance based on your location and travel mode.
- Automatic switching between walking and driving estimates when distance changes.
- Better synchronization between the app and Home Screen widgets.
- More helpful widget status messages when location information is unavailable or out of date.
- More reliable widget refresh behavior with improved battery efficiency.
- Clearer Live Activity countdown messaging as departure approaches.
- Improved schedule refresh feedback and current schedule information.
- Refined station, departure, and information screen wording and layout.
