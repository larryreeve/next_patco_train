App Subtitle:
------------
PATCO departures, at a glance


Promotional Text:
----------------
Save your everyday routes, see scheduled PATCO train departures, and know when to leave. Keep the next train departures handy with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.


Description:
-----------
Find the scheduled PATCO train that fits your trip.

Next PATCO Train shows upcoming scheduled departures and helps you decide which train you can catch. See scheduled arrival times, complete stop details, and leave-by guidance for walking or driving to the station.

Save routes with names like Work or Home, switch between them with a tap, and edit or reorder them whenever your routine changes. A reverse-route control makes planning the return trip simpler.

Clearer leave-by times and estimated station arrival times make departure planning easier to scan. When a reliable travel estimate is available, earlier trains you likely cannot catch are grouped out of the way, with an option to show them.

Use your location to find a nearby PATCO station. When you are at a station, view departures there without losing your saved route. Choose a future date to plan ahead, including special schedules with adjusted or added departures.

Keep your commute close at hand with Home Screen widgets and a Lock Screen widget showing your next three scheduled departures. Reverse your route, switch between driving and walking estimates, or refresh departures from the Home Screen widget. Follow a selected scheduled trip with a Lock Screen Live Activity.

Features:
- Upcoming scheduled PATCO departures
- Named saved routes with quick switching, editing, and reordering
- Reverse-route controls in the app and Home Screen widget
- Scheduled arrival times and complete stop details
- Walking and driving estimates with clearer leave-by guidance
- Expandable earlier departures when reliable travel estimates are available
- Nearby-station detection and at-station departures
- Future-date schedule planning
- Special-schedule notices, changes, and PDF access
- Home Screen and Lock Screen departure widgets
- Lock Screen Live Activities
- Links to official PATCO service information
- Siri Shortcuts support
- Automatic schedule updates

Next PATCO Train is an unofficial schedule app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PATCO or the Delaware River Port Authority.

Train times are based on published schedules and do not reflect real-time train operations. Schedules may change without notice. Check PATCO’s official website for service changes before traveling.

App Review Information - Notes:
------------------------------
Next PATCO Train is an unofficial, schedule-only app for PATCO train riders. It displays scheduled departures and arrivals, future-date schedules, special schedule notices and PDFs, official PATCO service-information links, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and optional Lock Screen Live Activities.

Version 1.3 adds named saved routes and streamlined route management. Users can save the selected route, switch routes using shortcuts, and edit or reorder saved routes from the route-options menu. Saved routes are stored locally. Saving or editing a route does not automatically select it for the current journey.

Location permission is optional. It supports nearby-station detection, at-station departures, walking or driving estimates, and station-aware route guidance. Scheduled departures remain available without location permission. Catchability and leave-by times are estimates, not guarantees. The app does not provide real-time train tracking and is not affiliated with PATCO or the Delaware River Port Authority.
